# Privacy Policy for Ranglume

**Effective date:✱ October 17, 2026

Ranglume ("the App") is developed by Karan Bhatia.

## Information We Collect

The current version of Ranglume does not collect, transmit, or sell personal information.

The App does not currently require:

- An account or sign-in
- Access to contacts
- Access to location
- Access to photos, camera, or microphone
- Advertising identifiers
- Analytics services
- Advertising services

Gameplay can be used without providing personal information.

## Data Stored on Your Device

Ranglume stores non-personal preferences (sound and music toggles, volume, haptics, reduced motion, colour assistance, and your selected level) locally on your device.
If Android Backup or Device Transfer is enabled on your device, Android may include these settings in your Google Account backup or transfer them to another device during setup.
Ranglume does not send or store these settings on Ranglume-operated servers.

## Third-Party Services

The current version of the App does not integrate third-party advertising, analytics, Firebase, or other services that collect or transmit user information.

## Children's Privacy

The current version of the App does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

If the App's data practices change, this Privacy Policy will be updated to accurately describe those practices. The updated policy will include a revised effective date.
