# Privacy Policy for Colour Memory Chess

**Effective date:** September 20, 2026

Colour Memory Chess ("the App") is developed by Karan Bhatia.

## Information We Collect

The current version of Colour Memory Chess does not collect, transmit, or sell personal information.

The App does not currently require:

- An account or sign-in
- Access to contacts
- Access to location
- Access to photos, camera, or microphone
- Advertising identifiers
- Analytics services
- Advertising services

Gameplay can be used without providing personal information.

## Data Stored on Your Device

The current version of the App does not intentionally store personal information on your device.

## Third-Party Services

The current version of the App does not integrate third-party advertising, analytics, Firebase, or other services that collect or transmit user information.

## Children's Privacy

The current version of the App does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

If the App's data practices change, this Privacy Policy will be updated to accurately describe those practices. The updated policy will include a revised effective date.
